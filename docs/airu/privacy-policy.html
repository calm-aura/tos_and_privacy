1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Calming Aura Studios LLC (“we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use the mobile application Panic Attack Relief Airu (the “App”). By using the App, you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

This policy is designed to work alongside Google Play and Apple App Store requirements and applicable privacy laws, including the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), where they apply.

2. Information we collect

We collect information in the categories below, depending on how you use the App.

2.1 Account and authentication

  • Identifiers: Email address, user ID issued by our authentication provider (Supabase), and (if you use them) identifiers from Apple or Google when you sign in with those services.
  • Credentials: Password (stored securely by our provider; we do not store your password in plain text).
  • Auth tokens: Session tokens stored on your device using secure on-device storage so you can stay signed in.

2.2 Profile and onboarding

  • Age group or similar age-band selections you choose during setup.
  • Anxiety-type preferences you select (for example, panic, social, or morning-related preferences).
  • Notification reminder preferences (times you choose for local daily reminders).

2.3 Sessions, mood, and progress (app content)

When you use core features, we may process:

  • Breathing session records (for example, technique used, anxiety type associated with a session, duration, cycles completed, whether Emergency Mode was used, and timestamps).
  • Optional mood check-in values after a session (for example, a simple 1–5 rating).
  • Derived progress metrics such as streaks, session counts, and calm-time summaries shown in the App.

Guests: If you use the App without an account, session and preference data may be stored locally on your device first. If you later create an account, we may sync that local data to our servers so it appears in your account.

2.4 Payments and subscriptions

  • Subscription status: We store subscription-related metadata needed to unlock features (for example, product identifier, platform, verification status, expiration, and a store transaction identifier).
  • Payment details: Purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We do not receive or store your full payment card number. Those platforms share limited purchase or entitlement information with us as required to operate subscriptions.

2.5 Device, technical, local preferences, and analytics

  • Device type, operating system, and app version as needed for support and compatibility.
  • Local preferences such as haptics settings and onboarding completion flags stored on your device.
  • Local notifications: If you enable reminders, scheduling is handled on your device. We do not use push notification providers to send marketing campaigns based on those reminders.
  • Usage analytics (Android): On Android builds, we use Google Firebase Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase) to understand app usage in aggregate — for example installs, sessions, screen views, and whether users open the paywall or complete a subscription. Events are designed to avoid collecting email addresses, names, or other direct contact identifiers as event parameters. Analytics is not currently enabled in the iOS build of the App.

We do not use third-party advertising SDKs or sell personal information for advertising. If that changes, we will update this policy.

2.6 Communications

  • If you email us (for example, at support@calmingaura.net), we process the content of your message and your contact details to respond.

2.7 Health and wellness context

The App is a wellness and self-help breathing tool. Optional mood ratings and anxiety-type selections are used only to operate features you choose (for example, recommendations and progress). The App is not a medical device and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease. Do not use the App as a substitute for professional care.

3. How we use your information

We use information to:

  • Provide, operate, and improve the App (sessions, progress, scenarios, Emergency Mode, and related features), including understanding product usage via analytics on Android.
  • Create and secure your account, and authenticate you.

  • Sync guest session data after you create an account (when applicable).

We do not sell your personal information. We do not show ads in the App.
