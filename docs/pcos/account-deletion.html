App: PCOS: Symptom & Period Tracker

Request account and associated data deletion

An app account is optional and is used for features such as cross-device sync. Subscribing to Premium does not require an app account. Premium is managed through your Google Play or Apple ID subscription settings.

To request deletion of your optional app account and associated synced data, email:

support@calmingaura.net

Use subject line: PCOS account deletion request

Include the email address used to sign in (Google or Apple), so we can locate the account.

What we delete

  • Your optional app account credentials and identifiers we hold for sync
  • Synced symptom, cycle, meds, labs, measurements, prep, and AI summary records associated with that account on our sync backend

What you should do in the app and stores

  • On your device, use Settings → Delete my data to wipe local logs on that phone (this does not cancel a store subscription).
  • To stop Premium billing, cancel the subscription in Google Play or Apple ID subscription settings. We cannot cancel store billing from this page.

Timing

We aim to complete account and associated synced-data deletion within 30 days of a verified request, except where law requires longer retention of limited records.
